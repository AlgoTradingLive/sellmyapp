Privacy Policy

SellMyApp · Last updated: August 21, 2026

SellMyApp ("we", "our", "the app") is a marketplace that lets people list, discover, and connect over running Android/iOS apps for sale. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

WhatWhen
Name and email addressWhen you sign up (email/password or Google Sign-In)
App listing details (title, description, price, category, screenshots, Play Store link)When you create a listing to sell an app
Contact numberWhen you provide it on a listing so buyers can reach you
Chat messagesWhen you message a buyer or seller in-app
Uploaded screenshots/imagesWhen you add photos to a listing
Public developer contact email from a Play Store listingOnly when you use the "Verify Ownership" feature on your own listing

How We Use This Information

  • To create and manage your account, and let you log in
  • To show your listings to other users and let buyers contact you
  • To deliver in-app chat messages between buyers and sellers
  • To verify that a seller genuinely owns the app they're listing, by sending a one-time code to that app's public Play Store developer email
  • To improve and maintain the app

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services We Use

SellMyApp is built on a few trusted services that process data on our behalf, only for the purposes described above:

  • Firebase (Google) — account login, and storing listings, chat messages, and verification records
  • Cloudinary — hosting the screenshots/images you upload for a listing
  • EmailJS — sending the one-time ownership verification code by email
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method, if you choose it

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on their infrastructure.

Data Sharing

Your listing details (title, description, price, screenshots, contact info you choose to add) are visible to anyone using the app, since that's the purpose of a public marketplace listing. Chat messages are only visible to the buyer and seller in that conversation — no one else can read them.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your account and listing data for as long as your account is active. You can delete a listing at any time from "My Listings" in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

SellMyApp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
